## Assessing Legal Argumentation in Generated Judgments: A Toulmin-Based Evaluation Framework

This repository contains the results and annotated datasets described in the paper 'Assessing Legal Argumentation in Generated Judgments: A Toulmin-Based Evaluation Framework'

* results_gpt-5_5.csv - the dataset and associated prompts used in the experiment, alongside the output and results of GPT-5.5.
* toulmin_score_sheet_annotator_A.xlsx - The manually crafted Toulmin models for 100 random samples of the dataset (random_state=42), alongside the annotations for the GPT-5.5 output, created and annotated by annotator A.
* toulmin_score_sheet_annotator_B.xlsx - The first 10 Toulmin models created by annotator A, annotated by annotator B.
